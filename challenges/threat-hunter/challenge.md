# Summary

Investigate potential threat actor activity using a SIEM.

# Rationale

This exercise has two goals:

* It helps us to understand what to expect from you as Threat Hunter.
* It helps you get a feel for what it would be like to work at Teleport, as
  this exercise aims to simulate our day-as-usual and expose you to the type of
  work we're doing here.

We believe this technique is not only better, but also is more fun compared to
whiteboard/quiz interviews so common in the industry. It's not without the
downsides - it could take longer than traditional interviews.

[Some of the best teams use coding challenges](https://sockpuppet.org/blog/2015/03/06/the-hiring-post/) — and we apply the same philosophy to non-coding roles.

We appreciate your time and are looking forward to hack on this project
together.

# Interview Process

## Threat Hunt Plan

After the kickoff call, you will have 3 days to submit a written plan for you threat hunt. You'll be given direction during the kickoff on the types of threats you'll be looking for. Your plan can use a format of your choosing, and should be limited to 1-2 pages.

The plan should include research on the designated threats, hypotheses, and TTPs for which you'll hunt in the simulated SIEM environment.

## During the Interview

You will join a 1 hour call, during which you'll be given access to a web application, which simulates a SIEM environment, with similar query features to Panther.

During this call, you will share you screen, and the call will be recorded to assist in the evaluation of your performance. You'll drive the call by executing your threat hunt, and your interviewer will ask questions throughout regarding your thinking and strategies.

# Report

After you complete the challenge, you will write a post-mortem report covering:

* What happened in the simulated environment
* The timeline of key events
* Suggested next steps
* Suggested detection strategies

The panel will review your report alongside your plan and interview recording and
privately/anonymously submit +1/-2 to the hiring manager.

## Areas of Focus

We will be evaluating the following:

* How well you aree able to research the designated threats and articulate a clear plan for the threat hunt
* How effectively you are able to navigate the SIEM environment and adapt to an unfamilar toolset
* The logic of your connections and ability to investigate potential threads through different log sources.

## Pitfalls and Gotchas

* Use of AI. Don't outsource your thinking to an AI. 
* Don't spend too much time on dead ends, there may be other more promising threads and you can come back to things.
* Taking notes. Make sure you have all of the information you need to write a comprehensive report after the fact.
* It's OK to ask the interview team questions; we'll do our best to provide hints or help steer the investigation if it's going in the wrong direction.
